2. Information we collect

Through our application form

Our application form asks for:

  • your full name
  • your email address
  • your business website
  • what your business sells
  • your average client value
  • who your ideal customer is
  • how you currently get clients
  • whether you have used cold email before

Your answers stay in your browser while you fill in the form. They are only passed on when you reach the booking step: the booking calendar from Calendly is then loaded with your answers included in its web address, so your booking is filled in for you (see section 7). If you close the form before that step, your answers are not sent to us or to Calendly.

The first two questions (whether your average customer is worth more than $3,000 and whether you have a B2B offer) are only checked in your browser to see if the service can be a fit. Your answers to them are not sent to us or to Calendly.

When you book a call

When you book, Calendly receives the details of your booking, such as the time you choose, your form answers and anything else you add. We receive these booking details from Calendly.

During calls

We note what you tell us about your business during calls. If a call is recorded and transcribed, we also process the recording, the transcript and AI-generated notes, as described in section 5.

When you work with us

If you become a client, we also process the business information needed to run your campaigns, such as details about your offer, target market and campaign materials, as well as our communication with you.

When you email us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

When you visit this website

When you visit, our hosting provider processes technical information about your visit, such as your IP address and the page you request. See section 6.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to:

  • assess whether your company qualifies for our service
  • schedule calls you book with us and prepare for them
  • understand your business and its target market
  • follow up on the consultation you asked for, our offer and a possible collaboration
  • onboard approved clients
  • build and manage cold email campaigns for approved clients
  • communicate with you about appointments and the service
  • deliver this website, keep it secure and fix technical problems
  • meet legal obligations and protect our legal rights where necessary

4. Emails we send you

If you book a call or meeting with us, we email you in connection with it. This can include booking confirmations and reminders, as well as follow-ups about the consultation you asked for, our offer and a possible collaboration. If you become a client, we also email you about the service.

We only send these emails to people who have booked a call or meeting with us. We do not run a newsletter or any other general marketing email list. If you don’t want further follow-ups, reply to our email or write to support@adelvo-global.ae.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Call recording & AI transcription

We may want to record and transcribe calls with you, such as strategy and onboarding calls. For this we use Calendly’s AI note-taking tool (Calendly Notetaker). It joins the video meeting, records it, creates a transcript and produces AI-generated notes, such as a summary and action items.

Booking a call does not mean you agree to it being recorded or transcribed. You can tell us before or during the call that you don’t want this. We will then not record or transcribe the call, or stop doing so, and the call can go ahead without it.

What this includes

  • the recording of the conversation, including your voice
  • the names of the participants
  • the transcript of what is said
  • AI-generated notes, such as summaries and action items
  • any information about you or your business that you share during the call

Why we use it

  • to document the information you give us during the call
  • to onboard you as a client
  • to plan your campaign strategy
  • to understand your offer and ideal customer profile
  • to deliver the service internally

Who has access

Recordings, transcripts and notes are available to our team members who need them for the purposes above. They are processed and stored by Calendly, which uses third-party AI providers to create transcripts and notes. You can read more in Calendly’s information about AI. The video meeting service used for the call, shown in your booking confirmation, also processes the call to provide the meeting.

How long we keep recordings, transcripts and notes is explained in section 10.

6. Website hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States). Vercel delivers the website through its global network, so your request may be handled in a data center near you.

When you visit a page or load a file from this website, Vercel processes technical information about the request, such as:

  • your IP address
  • the date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • the referring web address, if your browser sends it
  • browser, device and operating system information (user agent)
  • technical details of the response, such as status codes

This is needed to deliver the website to you, to protect it against misuse and attacks, and to find and fix errors. Vercel keeps this technical data for limited periods that Vercel sets; we don’t set a fixed period ourselves. Based on these requests, Vercel’s dashboard may show us aggregated usage information, such as how many requests the website received. See the Vercel Privacy Notice.

We have not added Vercel Web Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights or any other analytics or tracking script to this website, and the website does not send analytics events. Your form answers are not part of the requests sent to Vercel.

We use GitHub to store the website’s code and to deploy it to Vercel. This website does not send your form answers or visit data to GitHub.

7. Tools and service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run our website, application, booking and client process:

  • Calendly is used to schedule calls and, where you don’t object, to record and transcribe them (see section 5). When you reach the booking step, your form answers are included in the web address of the Calendly booking calendar, so Calendly receives them when the calendar loads and uses them to fill in your booking. For that processing, the provider is responsible, and its own privacy notice also applies.

8. Cookies and browser storage

Our own website pages do not set cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or Hotjar. The website’s font is stored on and loaded from our own website, not from Google Fonts or another font service.

The video player stores one setting in your browser’s local storage once you have watched the video to the end, so you can skip through it on later visits. This setting stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing your browser’s site data.

Calendly: when you reach the booking step, the Calendly booking calendar is loaded. Calendly may set its own cookies, including non-essential ones, and may show its own cookie banner where you can manage your choices. See the Calendly Privacy Notice.

9. International processing

We are based in the United Arab Emirates. Most of our service providers are based in the United States, and your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live:

  • Vercel is a US company and delivers this website through data centers in different countries (see section 6).
  • Calendly states that it stores user and invitee data in data centers in the United States. Its AI features use third-party AI providers.
  • Airtable stores data in the United States by default.

Data protection laws in these countries may differ from the laws where you live. We use established providers, share only what is needed for the purposes in this policy and limit access within our team. If you have questions about how your information is protected when it is processed abroad, please contact us.

10. How long we keep information

  • Application and booking details, and recordings, transcripts and AI notes of a call: we delete these 60 days after the call they relate to, unless one of the exceptions below applies.
  • If you become a client: we keep the information we need to deliver the service for as long as we need it for that purpose, and then delete it unless one of the exceptions below applies.
  • Technical data from website visits: kept by Vercel for limited periods that Vercel sets (see section 6).
  • Other information, such as emails you send us: kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this policy.

Deleting your information earlier

You don’t have to wait. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing support@adelvo-global.ae.

Exceptions

We keep specific information longer only where this is necessary:

  • to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations, for example invoices and records of approved software costs
  • to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, for example in a dispute

In these cases we keep only the information needed for that purpose, only for as long as it is needed, and then delete it. If we can’t delete something you asked us to delete, we will tell you why.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as limiting access to people who need it to deliver the service. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

12. Your requests

You can email support@adelvo-global.ae to ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your personal information
  • stop sending you follow-up emails
  • not record or transcribe a call with you

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under the data protection laws that apply to you. We will handle your request in line with applicable law. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and in some cases we may need to keep certain information, as explained in section 10.
