Adelvo Global FZ-LLC is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at support@adelvo-global.ae.

2. Information we collect

Through our application and booking process

When you apply for our service and book a call, we may collect:

  • your full name
  • your email address
  • your website
  • what your business sells
  • your average client value
  • whether your average customer is worth more than $3,000
  • whether your company is B2B
  • your ideal customer profile (ICP)
  • how you currently get clients
  • whether you have used cold email before
  • any other information you choose to give us when booking or during a call

When you work with us

If you become a client, we also process the business information needed to run your campaigns, such as details about your offer, target market and campaign materials, as well as our communication with you.

When you email us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to:

  • assess whether your company qualifies for our service
  • prepare for calls you book with us
  • understand your business and its target market
  • onboard approved clients
  • build and manage cold email campaigns for approved clients
  • send booking confirmations and reminders
  • communicate with you about appointments and the service

4. What we don’t do

We do not currently use your information for general promotional email marketing. We email you about your application, your booked calls and the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Call recording & transcription

Calls with us may be recorded and transcribed. This includes strategy, onboarding and other calls about the service. We let you know about this before or at the start of a recorded call.

We use recordings and transcripts to:

  • document the information you give us during the call
  • onboard you as a client
  • plan your campaign strategy
  • understand your offer and ideal customer profile
  • deliver the service internally

Recordings and transcripts are kept as described in section 9. If you have questions about a recording, contact us at support@adelvo-global.ae.

6. Tools and service providers

We use a small number of third-party tools to run our application, booking and client process:

  • Calendly is used to schedule calls. When you reach the booking step, the information you entered in our application form (such as your name, email and answers) is passed to Calendly to fill in your booking. Calendly processes your booking details and sends confirmations and reminders. Calendly Privacy Notice
  • Airtable is used to store relevant customer and business information, such as application details and client records. Airtable Privacy Policy

For approved clients, we also use cold email software and infrastructure providers (for example for domains, mailboxes and sending) to run the campaigns. We share with them only what is needed to operate your campaigns.

These providers are independent companies. We do not control their own privacy practices, and their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Cookies

This website itself does not set analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or Hotjar.

Two third-party services are loaded by the website:

  • Calendly: when you reach the booking step, a Calendly booking calendar is loaded. Calendly may set its own cookies, including non-essential ones, and may show its own cookie banner where you can manage your choices. See the Calendly Privacy Notice.
  • Google Fonts: the website’s font is loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver the font, Google receives technical information such as your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.

8. International processing

Some of our service providers operate in, or store data in, countries outside the United Arab Emirates, including the United States. This means your information may be processed in those countries under the provider’s own terms and safeguards.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

For applicants and clients taking part in the free service, relevant application and CRM information is generally kept for the 60-day service period. Some information may be kept longer where reasonably necessary, for example:

  • if you continue working with us under a separate paid agreement
  • to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations
  • to handle questions, complaints or disputes
  • as part of legitimate business records, such as records of approved software costs and invoices

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous within a reasonable time.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as limiting access to people who need it to deliver the service. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

11. Your requests

You can email support@adelvo-global.ae to ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your personal information

We will handle your request subject to applicable legal requirements. In some cases we may need to keep certain information, for example to meet legal obligations, and we may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we start using a new tool. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed.
