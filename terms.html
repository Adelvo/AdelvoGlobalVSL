3. Free 60-day service

Approved clients pay no agency management fee for the first 60 days of the service. During this free period there is:

  • no setup fee

  • no obligation to continue after the free period
  • no minimum paid contract after the free period

The only costs you pay are the actual software and infrastructure costs needed to run your campaigns, as explained in section 5.

4. Start of the 60-day service period

The 60-day free service period begins on the day mailbox/domain warm-up begins, not on the day your first campaign email is sent.

Before new mailboxes can send cold email safely, they need to be “warmed up”. Warm-up typically takes about 30 days. This means you should not expect 60 full days of live email sending. Part of the 60 days is used to prepare everything properly.

The free service period may include:

  • infrastructure setup
  • domain and mailbox setup
  • mailbox warm-up
  • targeting
  • lead sourcing
  • copywriting
  • campaign setup
  • testing
  • campaign launch
  • optimization

We will let you know when warm-up has started, so you know the date your 60-day period began.

10. Cold email and outreach risks

Cold email comes with risks that cannot be fully removed, even when campaigns are run carefully. For example:

  • emails may land in spam folders or be filtered
  • domains or mailboxes may lose reputation, be blocklisted or need replacing
  • email or software providers may limit, suspend or close accounts
  • some recipients may reply negatively or complain
  • deliverability and reply rates can change over time

We take reasonable steps to reduce these risks, but we cannot control how email providers, software providers or recipients respond.
