No guaranteed results

Adelvo Global FZ-LLC (“Adelvo Global”, “we”, “us”) does not guarantee:

  • a specific number of leads

This applies to the free 60-day service and to any paid service agreed later.

What results depend on

Results from cold email can vary widely. They depend on factors such as:

  • your offer and how clearly it solves a problem
  • your pricing
  • product-market fit
  • your ideal customer profile (ICP)
  • the size of your market
  • competition
  • your sales process
  • how quickly and well leads are followed up
  • your ability to close deals
  • your case studies and social proof
  • email deliverability
  • general campaign and market conditions

Many of these are in your hands, not ours. Our work is aimed at creating conversations and opportunities. Turning them into clients is part of your sales process.
