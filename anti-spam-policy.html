Anti-Spam Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Adelvo Global FZ-LLC

Adelvo Global FZ-LLC is committed to responsible, transparent, and legally compliant email communication. We maintain strict standards to prevent spam, misleading messages, and unauthorized use of our domains.

1. Targeted B2B Outreach

We conduct personalized business-to-business (B2B) cold email outreach exclusively in the United States and in other jurisdictions where such communication is legally permitted.

Our outreach is directed at carefully selected businesses and relevant decision-makers whose professional responsibilities and business needs align with our services.

We do not engage in indiscriminate mass emailing, deceptive outreach, or unsolicited commercial communications that violate applicable laws.

2. Permission-Based Marketing

Our newsletters, promotional subscriptions, and recurring marketing communications are sent only to individuals who have explicitly opted in to receive them.

We respect recipient preferences and do not add individuals to subscription-based marketing lists without their consent.

3. Opt-Out and Unsubscribe

All commercial outreach includes clear and accessible opt-out language.

Recipients can request to stop receiving further communications at any time by replying to our emails.

We promptly honor opt-out requests, maintain suppression lists, and ensure that recipients who have opted out are not contacted again for marketing purposes.

4. Compliance and Transparency

We follow applicable email marketing regulations, including the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act and relevant privacy laws.

Our communications use accurate sender information, transparent subject lines, and appropriate identification of commercial intent.

We prohibit phishing, fraudulent communications, misleading claims, and unauthorized use of our email infrastructure.

5. Domain and Sender Responsibility

This policy applies to all business email domains operated by Adelvo Global FZ-LLC, including adelvo-growth.com and adelvoglobal.com.

Employees, contractors, affiliates, and third-party service providers acting on our behalf must comply with this policy.

6. Complaints and Enforcement

We investigate complaints regarding unwanted or inappropriate email communications and take corrective action where necessary.

Our objective is to maintain professional communication standards, protect recipients, and prevent abuse of our email infrastructure.
